Designed for practice

Not one-and-done.

A completed activity remains useful. PolyLinux varies the target data so the method stays familiar while the work remains fresh.

↻

Meaningfully repeatable

New target data makes another attempt feel like practice, not replay.

✓

Spaced over time

A successful submission can count once per activity per day.

◇

Breadth encouraged

Up to five successful days per activity count toward recognition.

Sustained practice earns recognition

Your belt reflects the work you put in.

Every ten successful lab-day submissions advances your PolyLinux belt. A maximum of five successful days from any one activity counts toward advancement, encouraging both repetition and breadth.

Training pathways help you decide what to practice. Belt advancement reflects successful practice across the full activity catalog, not completion of a prescribed route.
